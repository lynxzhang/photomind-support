简体中文

PhotoMind for Mac 是本机照片与视频搜索、整理工具。无需注册账号，没有广告或开发者运营的分析服务，不向开发者上传照片、视频、搜索词、人物特征或相册索引。搜索用的场景模型在第一次需要时下载：macOS 26 及更新版本从 Apple 的 App Store 服务器下载；macOS 14–25 从开发者使用的模型托管服务下载同一套模型文件；macOS 26 及更新版本上如果拿不到 Apple 托管的模型包，也会改从这个托管服务下载同一套文件。下载只请求模型文件，不附带照片、视频、搜索词、设备标识或账号信息；托管服务会收到提供下载所需的连接信息（例如 IP 地址），按其自身规则处理。下载后的文件在本机校验，可离线使用。

经你授权，应用使用 Apple PhotoKit 访问系统照片图库中允许访问的照片、视频及拍摄日期、尺寸、时长、收藏状态和已有位置等元数据。照片访问可在 macOS 系统设置中更改。应用对本机可取得的内容进行分析，不能保证所有云端资产已经准备完成。Apple 可能按你的 Photos 和 iCloud 设置下载原片或管理缓存；这些系统服务由 Apple 提供。

Finder 文件仅在你通过文件选择器或拖入主动选择后读取。导入会将支持的照片与视频复制到系统照片图库，原文件保持不变；已导入的副本由 Photos 管理，并可能随 iCloud 照片设置同步。取消导入会停止后续批次，已经完成的导入会保留。

在“文件夹与移动硬盘”里，应用只读取你选择的文件夹或移动硬盘中的照片文件，在本机计算文件指纹和画面特征来找出重复与相似的照片；文件夹的访问授权（安全书签）和扫描结果保存在应用的本机私有存储，不会上传。删除这些文件需要你确认，能放进废纸篓的会放进废纸篓；没有废纸篓的磁盘会先提示文件将被永久删除。在该页面选择“不再扫描这个文件夹”，应用会停止该次扫描，删除对应的访问授权和扫描索引；正在读取的文件结束后释放系统访问权限。

识别文字、图像特征、抽样视频分析、资产标识、搜索索引、标签、命名地点及审核决定保存在应用的本机私有存储。地点功能使用媒体已有坐标，不请求实时定位，不执行在线逆地理编码。

人物功能在本机检测人脸并生成相似匹配用的数值特征（不是照片，但可以用来认出同一个人），保存你添加的姓名、昵称、已确认样本与纠正决定；只使用你手动确认的人脸作为参考。App 不会另存从你相册裁出的人脸图片；只有在你导入人物文件时，才会保存文件附带的小头像（每人一张，不超过 200 KB），删除该人物时一并删除。这些数据不会传送给开发者或第三方。你可以在人物管理中删除标记。这项功能用于个人相册整理，不用于身份验证，结果可能有误。

你可以把人物分享给家人，或者用“备份到文件”把全部人物存成一个文件。人物文件里有名字、别称和人脸特征，不包含相册原片或视频；分享时默认附带每人一张约 192×192 像素的头像（从本机照片裁出，分享前可以关掉“附带头像”），备份文件总是包含全部人物和头像。人物文件没有加密：发给谁、存在哪里由你决定，拿到文件的人能看到其中的名字和头像，也能用人脸特征在自己的相册里认出这些人。请只分享给你信任的人，并妥善保管备份。制作文件时的临时副本在分享或保存结束后删除；导入时只读取名字、别称、人脸特征和头像。

高级版是一次性 App 内购买，付款由 Apple 处理；App 只通过 StoreKit 在本机读取经过 Apple 签名验证的购买状态，开发者收不到你的 Apple 账户、付款或购买记录，免费清理额度的已用量只记在本机。“隐私照片”页默认模糊显示，查看时由 macOS 用触控 ID 或登录密码验证，App 只得到是否通过的结果；同样的照片在其他页面和“照片”App 里照常显示。“完整分析 iCloud 内容”要你主动开启，开启后由系统从你自己的 iCloud 下载只在云端的原件并在本机分析，不经过开发者的服务器。照片提醒和“按月整理”提醒是 macOS 在本机安排的通知，不经过任何服务器，也不显示照片或识别出的文字。

缩略图保留在内存中。压缩、视频裁剪、Live Photo 静态转换、保存、分享或拖出时，应用可能建立临时媒体文件，并在任务结束或下次启动时清理遗留的临时媒体文件。你主动保存或导入的副本成为系统照片图库中的独立内容，或由你选择的接收应用保管。仅关闭应用不会删除已保存的副本。

删除照片或视频需要你的明确确认，并遵循 Apple Photos 的规则。删除可能同步到开启 iCloud 照片的其他设备；恢复、最近删除和实际释放空间由系统管理。导出副本本身不修改原件；若转换流程提供删除原件选项，仍需要另行确认。

偏好、索引与人物数据保存在本机。移除人物标记或其他整理记录可通过相应管理界面进行。macOS 将应用移入废纸篓不一定同时移除沙盒数据；可在应用菜单中选择“清除本机数据…”：应用会先退出，下次打开前清除分析结果、人物标记、搜索记录、文件夹列表和诊断文件，保留模型文件、高级版购买状态与已用免费额度。系统照片图库原件和主动保存的副本不随索引清理而删除。系统备份、Time Machine 或你自行创建的备份按你的设置管理，清除当前记录不会自动清除旧备份。

你主动使用系统分享、拖出、隐私/支持网页或邮件时，接收应用、浏览器、Apple 与邮箱服务商会按各自规则处理你选择的内容和必要连接信息。网页托管服务可能接收访问该网页所需的连接信息；应用不会向该网页发送相册内容。系统交给应用的崩溃和卡顿报告只保存在本机（最多 8 份），仅在你主动选择附带诊断信息反馈时加入邮件草稿，发送前可以查看和修改。报告含调用堆栈、应用和系统版本、设备类型，不含照片、文件名或搜索内容。通过支持邮件提供的信息只用于处理你的请求，请不要发送无关照片或敏感数据。开发者不能删除其他服务提供方或你自行备份的数据。

English

PhotoMind for Mac searches and organizes photos and videos on your Mac. It requires no account, contains no advertising or developer-operated analytics, and does not upload photos, videos, queries, face features or library indexes to the developer. The scene models used for search are downloaded the first time they are needed: on macOS 26 and later from Apple's App Store servers, and on macOS 14–25 from the model hosting service the developer uses, which serves the same model files. On macOS 26 and later, if the Apple-hosted pack is not available, the app downloads the same files from that hosting service instead. These requests ask only for model files and carry no photos, videos, queries, device identifiers or account information; the hosting service receives the connection data needed to serve the download (such as the IP address) and handles it under its own policies. Downloaded files are verified on your Mac and then work offline.

With your permission, PhotoMind uses Apple PhotoKit to access authorized content in the system Photos library, including capture dates, dimensions, duration, favorites and existing location metadata. You can change photo access in macOS System Settings. Analysis uses content available locally; cloud-only items are not assumed to be ready. Apple may download originals or manage caches according to your Photos and iCloud settings.

Files from Finder are read only after you choose them through the file picker or drag them into the app. Import copies supported photos and videos into the system Photos library and leaves the source files unchanged. Imported copies are managed by Photos and may sync through iCloud Photos. Cancelling stops later batches; imports already completed remain in Photos.

In Folders & Drives, PhotoMind reads only the photo files in the folders or drives you choose, and computes file fingerprints and visual features on your Mac to find duplicate and similar photos. Folder access grants (security-scoped bookmarks) and scan results stay in private local app storage and are not uploaded. Deleting these files requires your confirmation and moves them to the Trash where the drive has one; on a drive without a Trash, the app first tells you the files will be deleted permanently. Choosing Stop Scanning This Folder stops the scan and removes its stored access grant and scan index; any in-flight file read finishes before its system access scope is released.

Recognized text, visual features, sampled video analysis, asset identifiers, indexes, labels, named places and review decisions remain in private local app storage. Place labels use coordinates already present in media. PhotoMind does not request live location or perform online reverse geocoding.

People features detect faces and compute matching features locally: a series of numbers, not a picture, but one that can recognize the same person. The app stores the names, aliases, confirmed samples and corrections you provide. Only faces you manually confirm become reference samples. The app does not keep separate face images cut from your library; only when you import a people file does it keep the small portrait that came with it (one per person, up to 200 KB), and it deletes that portrait together with the person. These records are not sent to the developer or third parties, and labels can be removed in people management. This organizes your personal library; it is not identity authentication and may be incorrect.

You can share people with your family, or save everyone in one file with Back Up to Files. A people file contains names, nicknames and face features, and no original library photos or videos. When you share, it includes by default one portrait of about 192 × 192 pixels per person, cut from a photo on your Mac (you can turn off Include Portraits first); a backup always contains everyone and their portraits. People files are not encrypted: you decide whom to send them to and where to keep them, and anyone who gets a file can see the names and portraits and use the face features to recognize these people in their own library. Share only with people you trust and keep backups safe. The temporary copy made while preparing a file is deleted once sharing or saving ends, and importing reads only names, nicknames, face features and portraits.

Pro is a one-time in-app purchase and Apple handles the payment. The app only reads your purchase status on your Mac from StoreKit, verified by Apple’s signature; the developer does not receive your Apple Account, payment or purchase history, and how much of the free cleanup allowance you have used is recorded only on your Mac. Private Photos stays blurred until macOS confirms you with Touch ID or your login password, and the app only learns whether that succeeded; the same photos still appear elsewhere in the app and in Photos. Full iCloud Analysis runs only if you turn it on: the system then downloads originals that are only in your own iCloud so the app can analyze them on your Mac, without going through the developer’s servers. Photo reminders and the Month by Month reminder are notifications scheduled by macOS on your Mac; they never go through a server and never show a photo or recognized text.

Thumbnails are held in memory. Compression, video clipping, Live Photo still conversion, saving, sharing and dragging files out may create temporary media files, which are removed when their tasks finish or, after an interrupted session, on the next launch. Copies you choose to save or import remain as separate Photos assets or with your chosen receiving app. Closing PhotoMind does not remove saved copies.

Deleting media requires your explicit confirmation and follows Apple Photos rules. Deletion may sync to other devices through iCloud Photos; recovery, Recently Deleted and actual space reclamation are controlled by the system. Exporting a copy does not modify the original. If a conversion offers removal of originals, that requires separate confirmation.

Preferences, indexes and people records are stored locally. Use the relevant management screen to remove people labels or other organization records. Moving a Mac app to the Trash does not necessarily remove its sandbox data. Use Clear Local Data in the app menu: PhotoMind quits, then removes analysis results, people labels, saved and recent searches, folder lists and diagnostic files before the next launch. Downloaded models, Pro purchase status and the used free allowance are retained. Clearing an index does not delete originals or saved copies in Photos. System backups, Time Machine and copies you create follow your settings; removing current records does not automatically remove previous backups.

When you choose system sharing, drag files out, open privacy or support pages, or send email, the receiving app, browser, Apple and email provider handle your chosen content and necessary connection data under their own policies. Website hosts may receive connection data needed to serve those pages; PhotoMind does not send library content to them. Crash and hang reports supplied by the system stay on your Mac (at most the latest eight). They are added to a feedback email only when you choose to include diagnostics, and you can review the draft before sending. Reports contain call stacks, app and system versions and device type, without photos, file names or searches. Information you send to support is used to address your request. Please avoid unnecessary photos or sensitive information. The developer cannot delete data held by independent providers or in your own backups.
