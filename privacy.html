隐私政策 / Privacy policy

Updated · 2026-09-18

简体中文

拾光相册无需注册账号。照片与视频的画面分析、文字识别及搜索索引在你的设备上处理。当前版本没有广告、第三方统计或向我们服务器上传照片、视频与搜索词的功能。

经你授权，App 通过 Apple PhotoKit 读取所允许的照片、视频及其拍摄时间、尺寸、收藏状态和已有位置等元数据。你可授予全部或部分相册访问权限，并随时在系统设置中更改。

本机索引保存用于搜索的文字、图像特征、部分视频帧的分析结果、资产标识和审核决定。你添加的地点名称与照片已有位置仅在本机用于地点匹配。App 不请求实时定位。

App 不建立永久原片副本。缩略图缓存位于内存；导出视频片段会产生临时文件，分享或保存期间可能需要保留。系统获取 iCloud 内容可能使用移动数据并占用系统管理的本地空间；这些缓存由 Apple 管理，App 无法承诺立即释放。你主动保存的导出片段会成为相册中的新文件。

删除必须由你确认，并通过 Apple 的系统请求执行。删除可能同步到开启 iCloud 照片的其他设备。恢复与最终释放空间遵循系统相册的规则。

语言偏好、审核决定和搜索索引保存在 App 的本地存储。卸载 App 会移除其本地数据；系统相册中的原照片和视频不因卸载而删除。设备备份由你的系统设置控制。

English

PhotoMind requires no account. Visual analysis, text recognition and search indexing run on your device. This version contains no advertising or third-party analytics, and does not upload photos, videos or search queries to our servers.

With your permission, PhotoMind uses Apple PhotoKit to access selected or all photos and videos, together with metadata such as capture dates, dimensions, favorites and existing location information. You can change access at any time in system Settings. PhotoMind does not request live location access.

The local index stores recognized text, visual features, sampled video analysis, asset identifiers and review decisions. Place names you provide are matched to existing photo locations locally.

PhotoMind does not maintain permanent copies of originals. Thumbnail caches are held in memory. Video export creates temporary files while saving or sharing; clips you explicitly save become new library assets. Apple may download iCloud content using mobile data and system-managed storage. These system caches are controlled by Apple; PhotoMind cannot promise their immediate removal.

You must confirm deletion through Apple's system request. Deletions may sync across devices using iCloud Photos. Recovery and final storage reclamation follow the system Photos rules.

Language preferences, review decisions and the index stay in app storage. Uninstalling the app removes its local data, without deleting your original media in Photos. Device backups follow your system settings.
