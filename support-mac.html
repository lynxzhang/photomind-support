开始使用

需要 macOS 14 或更新版本。应用使用系统照片图库。首次打开时允许访问照片与视频；Finder 中的文件可通过“文件 → 导入照片与视频…”（⌘O）复制进照片图库，原文件不会移动。照片图库之外的照片可以在“清理 → 文件夹与移动硬盘”（⇧⌘O）里查重：选择文件夹或移动硬盘，找出完全相同、几乎相同和相似的照片，以及已经在照片图库里的副本。文件只在本机读取，不上传；删除前会确认，能放进废纸篓的会放进废纸篓，没有废纸篓的磁盘会先提示将永久删除。

按画面内容搜索需要一套场景模型（中文界面约 176 MB，其他语言约 273 MB），第一次用到时自动下载，之后可离线使用；macOS 26 及更新版本由 App Store 下载，macOS 14–25 由应用从模型托管服务下载并逐个文件校验。第一次准备取决于图库规模和设备性能，已经完成的内容可以先搜索。切到其他应用后可继续处理；电脑睡眠时停止工作，退出后可从保存的进度恢复，不安装常驻后台服务。

查找与整理

⌘1 打开查找，⌘2 打开清理，⌘F 聚焦搜索，⌘R 检查图库变化，⌘N 新建窗口，⌘, 打开设置。可以按画面、文字、日期或媒体类型搜索。视频结果来自抽样画面，不转写声音，也不覆盖每一帧。识别可能有误，请核对原图。

压缩、裁剪和静态转换会生成副本。清理候选需要你逐项审核，删除需要明确确认；iCloud 同步、恢复和释放空间遵循 Photos 规则。导入取消后，已完成的批次会保留。切换语言前请结束其他窗口的导入、编辑或审核流程。

没有结果或无法导出

先检查 macOS 系统设置中的照片权限、当前系统照片图库、准备进度和磁盘空间。iCloud 原片可能需要由系统先下载，断网时云端内容可能不可用。视频编解码、HDR 与元数据保留受系统和素材格式限制；失败不会被当作成功。请发送 macOS 版本、芯片类型、应用版本、发生步骤与错误文字；不用发送整个相册。

English

Requires macOS 14 or later. PhotoMind uses the system Photos library. Grant photo access, or use File → Import Photos and Videos… (⌘O) to copy supported Finder files into Photos. Source files stay unchanged. To find duplicate photos outside the library, use Clean → Folders & Drives (⇧⌘O) and choose folders or drives: it finds exact duplicates, near duplicates, similar photos and copies already in Photos. Files are read only on your Mac and never uploaded; deleting asks first and uses the Trash when the drive has one, otherwise it warns that files will be deleted permanently.

Searching by what is in a picture needs a scene model (about 176 MB for the Chinese interface, about 273 MB for other languages). It downloads automatically the first time it is needed and then works offline: from the App Store on macOS 26 and later, and on macOS 14–25 from the model host, with every file verified. Preparation time varies with the library and hardware. You can search completed items while more are being prepared. Work can continue while another app is active; it pauses during sleep and resumes from saved progress after reopening. There is no always-running background service.

Use ⌘1 for Find, ⌘2 for Clean, ⌘F for search, ⌘R to check library changes, ⌘N for another window and ⌘, for Settings. Search by scene, recognized text, dates or media type. Video search uses sampled frames, without audio transcription or every-frame coverage. Verify results against your originals.

Compression, clips and still conversions create copies. Cleanup candidates need your review and deletion requires confirmation. iCloud synchronization, recovery and reclaimed storage follow Photos rules. Completed import batches remain after cancellation. Finish imports, edits or review workflows in other windows before changing language.

If content is missing, check Photos permission, the system Photos library, preparation status and available storage. iCloud originals may need a system download and may be unavailable offline. Codec support, HDR and metadata preservation depend on the system and source. Contact support with the macOS version, chip, app version, steps and error text; do not send your entire library or unnecessary sensitive material.
